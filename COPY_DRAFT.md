## Research

### Oncology care pathways and prioritization
My doctoral research examines patient management and waiting-list administration in oncology through simulation and data- and process-mining approaches. The broader goal is to develop transparent, evidence-informed ways of studying prioritization and care delivery under resource constraints.

### Process mining and healthcare operations
I use process-oriented and data-driven approaches to understand how patients move through healthcare systems, identify variation in care pathways, and support the analysis of operational bottlenecks and resource constraints.

### Discrete-event simulation
Simulation provides a framework for exploring complex healthcare systems, comparing scenarios, and examining how changes in capacity, prioritization, and workflow may affect system behavior. The scope and interpretation of each model depend on the available data, assumptions, and validation stage.

### Digital health and institutional transformation
My professional work connects research with the implementation of health information systems, digital infrastructure, interoperability, and technology-enabled improvements in clinical and organizational settings.

## Doctoral research

### Simulation-Based Patient Management and Data- and Process-Mining-Driven Waiting-List Administration: An Applied Oncology Case Study

Original Spanish title: *Modelo de gestión de pacientes mediante simulación y administración de listas de espera basada en minería de datos y procesos: caso aplicado del paciente oncológico.*

This research develops an applied framework for studying oncology patient management through process analysis, waiting-list prioritization, and simulation. The project connects observed healthcare pathways with operational modeling and decision-support questions in cancer care.
