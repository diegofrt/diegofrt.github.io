About

Research connected to clinical and institutional realities.

I am a biomedical engineer and PhD candidate in Engineering and Sciences with the Industry at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile. I am currently a Visiting Researcher in the Department of Industrial and Operations Engineering at the University of Michigan from August 3 to December 18, 2026, working with Professor Mariel S. Lavieri.

I am also a researcher and Head of Digital Transformation at Chile’s National Cancer Institute. My work connects quantitative methods with healthcare delivery, information systems, and institutional leadership to study how health systems can be better understood, managed, and redesigned.

Doctoral research

Simulation-Based Patient Management and Data- and Process-Mining-Driven Waiting-List Administration

An applied oncology case study.

My doctoral research develops an applied framework for studying oncology patient management through process analysis, waiting-list prioritization, and simulation. The project connects observed healthcare pathways with operational modeling and decision-support questions in cancer care.

Original titleModelo de gestión de pacientes mediante simulación y administración de listas de espera basada en minería de datos y procesos: caso aplicado del paciente oncológico.
